# Clinical threshold references (participant report)

Values below are the **starting reference** for MedScreen participant-facing
classification. They must be reviewed periodically against current guidelines.

## Blood pressure (mmHg)

| Category | Systolic | Diastolic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Optimă | `<120` | `<80` | ESC/ESH 2018 hypertension guideline — optimal BP |
| Normală | `120–129` | `80–84` | ESC/ESH 2018 — high-normal / normal range |
| Normal-înaltă | `130–139` | `85–89` | ESC/ESH 2018 — high-normal / grade 1 pre-hypertension band |
| Hipertensiune grad 1 | `140–159` | `90–99` | ESC/ESH 2018 — hypertension grade 1 |
| Hipertensiune grad 2 | `160–179` | `100–109` | ESC/ESH 2018 — hypertension grade 2 |
| Hipertensiune grad 3 | `≥180` | `≥110` | ESC/ESH 2018 — hypertension grade 3 |
| Hipotensiune | `<90` | `<60` | ESC/ESH 2018 — low BP / symptomatic hypotension reference |

Classification uses the **most severe** applicable category when systolic and
diastolic fall in different bands.

## BMI (kg/m²)

| Category | Range | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subponderal | `<18.5` | WHO — underweight |
| Normal | `18.5–24.9` | WHO — normal weight |
| Supraponderal | `25–29.9` | WHO — overweight |
| Obezitate grad I | `30–34.9` | WHO — obesity class I |
| Obezitate grad II | `35–39.9` | WHO — obesity class II |
| Obezitate grad III | `≥40` | WHO — obesity class III |

## Blood glucose (mg/dL)

| Context | Normal | Prediabet / toleranță alterată | Sugestiv diabet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Á jeun (`fasting`) | `<100` | `100–125` | `≥126` |
| Postprandial / ocazional (`non_fasting`) | `<140` | `140–199` | `≥200` |

Sources: ADA Standards of Care — fasting and casual glucose cut-offs.

Hypoglycaemia attention threshold: `<70 mg/dL` (clinical safety flag).

## SpO₂ (%)

| Category | Range | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normală | `≥95` | WHO / clinical practice — normal room-air saturation |
| Ușor scăzută | `90–94` | Requires clinical correlation |
| Sever scăzută | `<90` | Hypoxaemia — requires prompt medical attention |

## Pulse (bpm)

| Category | Range | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | `60–100` | Standard adult resting heart rate (clinical practice) |
| Bradicardie | `<60` | Below normal resting range |
| Tahicardie | `>100` | Above normal resting range |

## Waist circumference (cm) — cardiometabolic risk (IDF)

| Sex | Normal | Risc crescut | Risc foarte crescut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bărbați | `<94` | `94–101.9` | `≥102` |
| Femei | `<80` | `80–87.9` | `≥88` |

Source: IDF waist circumference cut-offs for Europids / clinical practice.
